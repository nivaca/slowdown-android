<p>Slowdown plays audio slower or faster without changing its pitch. It is made for music: for practising a piece and learning it by ear, hearing a passage again and again at a speed one can follow. It is the Android version of py-slowdown, with the same engine and the same <i>.slx</i> files.</p>

<ul>
<li>Speed from 0.25× to 3×, changed while it plays, with the pitch kept, by the R3 engine of the Rubber Band Library. At 1× the file plays as it is, sample for sample.</li>
<li>The pitch up or down in semitones and cents, to tune a recording to an instrument; the formants can be kept.</li>
<li>Repeats a selection without a gap (hold a finger on the waveform and drag, or A and B), or the whole file; selections can be kept as loops with a name.</li>
<li>Bookmarks, with names, drawn on the waveform; they can be exported as text.</li>
<li>The waveform, darker where it has played; a tap goes there, a pinch zooms.</li>
<li>Knobs for the speed, the pitch and the volume, with a Boost for quiet recordings.</li>
<li>Reads MP3, FLAC, WAV and AIFF by itself, and AAC, Opus, Ogg and the sound of videos with Android's codecs.</li>
<li>Remembers each file: where it was left, how it was played, its bookmarks and loops, in a <i>.slx</i> file that py-slowdown also reads.</li>
<li>Works with a keyboard or a foot pedal that sends keys, and with the button of headphones.</li>
<li>English and Spanish.</li>
</ul>
